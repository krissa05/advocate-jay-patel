About Advocate Jay Patel

Committed to Justice.
Focused on Results.

With over 15 years of dedicated legal practice and more than 200 successful matters across Gujarat's courts, Advocate Jay Patel brings sharp legal acumen, deep procedural knowledge, and a results-driven mindset to every case. His practice spans criminal defence, civil litigation, family disputes, land revenue matters, and financial crime cases, making him one of the most versatile trial advocates practising across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar today.

He appears and practices in District & Sessions Courts at Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and across Gujarat, as well as the Gujarat High Court and specialised tribunals. Known for his direct, no-delegation approach, he personally oversees every critical legal move, from the first consultation to the final verdict, ensuring his clients receive focused, strategic and completely confidential representation at every stage of their matter.
